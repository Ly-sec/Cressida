## Cressida v0.2.0

**New**

- Record and send voice messages, with waveform seeking, playback speed and
  volume controls.
- Create polls, vote for one or multiple answers, and view results.
- Forward messages to up to five destinations, with an optional comment and
  emoji picker.
- Pin and unpin messages with rich previews and clickable pin notices.
- Open voice-channel chats without joining a call, with optional confirmation
  before joining.
- Choose interface sound sets and adjust their volume.
- Watch videos fullscreen with larger playback controls. Larger animated images
  are also supported.

**Improvements & fixes**

- Search results grouped by channel, with message previews and numbered pages.
- Readable timestamp mentions, plus exact dates and times on hover.
- Improved voice and screen sharing, including keeping calls during recoverable
  chat reconnects.
- Live server nicknames, clearer text and remembered member/profile panel
  visibility.
- Reordered message menus and GIF/sticker buttons. Revealed blocked or ignored
  messages can now be hidden again.
- Fixed misleading unread dots, mention names after editing, composer
  placeholder jumps and stale activity titles.
