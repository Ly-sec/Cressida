Cressida is a native desktop chat client for Linux and Wayland. Bring your
server conversations, threads, forum posts and DMs into a desktop interface you
can make your own.

## Start here

1. [Install Cressida](installation.md) with Nix, Guix or a source build.
2. [Sign in](sign-in.md) through your browser.
3. [Find your conversations](chat.md) and try a reply, an emoji or an image.
4. [Choose your appearance](appearance.md) and a palette that suits your
   desktop.

Want to look around first? Run `cressida --demo` for an offline preview using
sample conversations. Demo mode does not sign in or connect to your account.

## What is available?

Text chat, one-to-one and group DMs, threads, forum posts, rich profiles, image
uploads, native media viewing and voice chat are available. Desktop tray
integration, notifications and per-account appearance settings help it fit into
your session.

:::caution[Early development] Video calls, arbitrary file uploads and file
drag-and-drop are not available yet. Some settings and actions are incomplete.
See the [capabilities and FAQ](faq.md) before making Cressida your main client.
:::
