## Channels and unread activity

Choose a server from the rail, then a channel from its sidebar. Threads appear
under their parent channel with an L-shaped branch. Forum channels provide a
place to browse posts and their conversations.

Unread markers sit inside the channel sidebar. Mention counts appear on rail
icons and folders. Large counts use `99+`; hover a count to see its full value.
Muted channels and categories appear dimmer, while hovered, selected and
mentioned items remain readable.

Automatic read receipts are grouped while you read active conversations. Local
unread indicators update immediately. Explicit **Mark as Read** actions are sent
immediately.

## Use folders

Click a folder to expand or collapse it. An expanded folder keeps its servers
together on a shared background. Folder opening and closing follow the reduced
motion preference.

## Rearrange servers

Drag a server or folder in the rail to rearrange it:

- Drop near an item's edge to place it before or after that item.
- Drop a server onto the middle of another server to create a folder.
- Drop a server into a folder to group it there.
- Drag a server outside a folder's group to take it out.
- Drag a folder to move the whole group.

Insertion and highlight feedback show the target. Pause over a closed folder
while dragging to open it. Move toward a scrollable rail's edge to scroll. Press
**Escape** to cancel a drag.

Allow an organization change to finish saving before starting another drag.

## Threads and forum posts

Select a thread beneath its parent or open a forum post to read its
conversation. Available creation and management actions depend on the channel
and your access. Some management features are still incomplete; see
[Current capabilities](faq.md).
