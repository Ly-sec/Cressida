## Find a conversation

Use the Cressida logo at the top of the server rail to return to direct
messages. It restores your last valid DM. If you have no conversations, it opens
Friends. Friends and Message Requests also have their own entries in the
sidebar.

Use **Find or start a conversation** to find a person or conversation. The DM
list shows avatars, presence and available custom statuses. A person's server
tag can appear beside their name.

## Read a profile beside your chat

In a one-to-one DM, the right panel shows the recipient's profile. It can
include:

- A banner, avatar and presence indicator.
- Display name, username, pronouns and server tag.
- Custom status, mutual friend/server counts and account creation date.
- A biography and a **View Full Profile** button.

The chat header also shows the recipient's avatar and custom status. Use the
header's panel toggle to show or hide the side panel. At narrow window widths,
Cressida hides the optional panel to keep room for the conversation.

Profile information depends on what is available to your account. Private or
unavailable profiles keep a basic identity visible. If loading fails, use the
offered retry action.

## Group conversations

Group DMs show the participant list instead of a single-person profile panel.
Opening a group does not replace its participants with one member's profile.

For message formatting and replies, see [Chat and Markdown](../chat/).
